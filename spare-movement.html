How this works

Search above for the spare or installed device you want to move and click Select — its name, category and From Location fill in automatically below, along with availability. Just enter the Quantity (for spare stock) and To Location, then submit.

Moving from Spare Stock decreases the quantity at the From location and increases it at the To location, creating a stock record at the destination if one doesn't exist yet. Blocked if there isn't enough stock at the source.

Moving an installed device is always quantity 1 and what happens next depends on the device and the To Location (a live hint appears on the form once both are picked):

• NCMC / QR device → another station, or a Zone (Zone 1/2/3): the device is always fully relocated. Its row is removed from the source sheet and re-created as a brand-new asset record at the destination with a new Asset ID (all its other details — serial number, MID/TID, manufacturer, install date, etc. — carry over). If it's headed to a Zone, its status also flips to "Non Operational" / "Under Repair" at the destination. Spare Stock / Zone Store is never touched by these moves — it only ever holds genuine spare-parts stock added manually.

• AG component (FEIG reader / QR scanner) → anywhere: marked unavailable on the original AG gate record instead of being relocated, since it's one component embedded in a shared gate row, not a standalone record — its Availability is set to "No" and a note is added to Remarks. A matching spare stock entry is created (or topped up) at the To location.
